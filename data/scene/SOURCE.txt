LAMDA Scene MIML dataset

Official dataset page:
https://www.lamda.nju.edu.cn/data_MIMLimage.ashx

Required dataset acknowledgment/citation:
Z.-H. Zhou and M.-L. Zhang. Multi-instance multi-label learning with
application to scene classification. Advances in Neural Information
Processing Systems 19 (NIPS 2006), MIT Press, 2007, pp.1609-1616.

The official provider offers this dataset for academic use and requests
acknowledgment/citation. See the official page for its usage terms.

Contents:
miml data.mat: all 2000 official processed bags, targets, and class_name.
scene_full_pointnet.npz: the same 2000 bags in the existing reproducible
1500/500 split, seed 42. Original images are not needed by this feature-based
training project and are not bundled.

Original source sample k corresponds to MATLAB bags{k,1} and targets(:,k).
Each bag has 9 instances with 15 features each. Label order:
desert, mountains, sea, sunset, trees.
